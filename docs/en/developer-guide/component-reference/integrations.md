---
title: Integrations
description: Compositor, login, lock and portal integrations.
---

`argvus-hyprland` packages compositor configuration; `argvus-portal` packages XDG portal integration; `argvus-greeter` integrates greetd; `argvus-lock` integrates Hyprlock; `argvus-theme-splash` integrates session-loading visuals; and `argvus-splash` integrates Plymouth boot visuals.
